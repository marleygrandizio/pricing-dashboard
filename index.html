How to read the numbers

Representative monthly benchmark uses one representative offer per competitor: VEEPS All Access monthly and nugs standard monthly. Its range is $12.99–$19.99; mean and median are both $16.49. The nugs amount is promotional, with $14.99 listed after the first year.

All-offer view includes every priced offer in the JSON: monthly, annual, and event prices remain in separate units. Add-ons are not in the dataset's standalone offer set. Missing prices are not imputed.

Availability reflects the research notes. Prices are snapshots, not live quotes, and may have changed.
